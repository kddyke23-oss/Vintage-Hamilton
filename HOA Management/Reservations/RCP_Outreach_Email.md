Subject: Vintage@Hamilton — Consolidating Clubhouse & Pickleball Reservations Through Our Resident Portal

Hi [RCP contact],

As you manage reservations on behalf of the Vintage@Hamilton board, we wanted to loop you in on a change we're making: we're moving all clubhouse and pickleball court reservation requests into our resident portal, so residents have a single place to check availability and submit requests instead of two separate calendars.

Here's what we're proposing, and where we'd like your input:

1. Routing. Every reservation request submitted through our portal would be forwarded to you with the full booking details (date, time, space, resident name/unit, headcount, and whether the resident flagged it as a private event). You'd apply your existing fee criteria to determine whether a fee applies.

2. Billing. For anything that requires a fee, we'd like you to continue contacting the resident directly to collect the fee and security deposit, as you do today — we're not looking to take that over, just to stop requiring residents to submit the request twice.

3. Closing the loop — a few options, happy to discuss which works best for your team:
   - Portal accounts for your staff. We could set up a limited-access account for whoever on your team handles these requests, so they can see pending requests and mark them approved / needs billing / cancelled directly in our system — no emails back and forth needed for routine cases. We could also list a contact for this in our resident directory, so people know who to reach for billing questions.
   - API/webhook. If you have a way to receive requests and send status updates programmatically, we're happy to build to that instead.
   - Email-based, as a fallback. If neither of the above fits, a simple structured email in each direction works as a starting point.

4. Your resident-facing calendar. Since our portal will be the single place residents check availability, we'd like to turn off resident access to the booking calendar on your site, to avoid two conflicting sources of availability, while keeping your internal/billing access intact.

5. No-shows / unpaid fees. How do you currently handle a case where a fee or deposit goes unpaid — is there a standard window before a booking is cancelled? We'd like to mirror that so our calendar doesn't hold a slot indefinitely.

6. Fee schedule. Separately, the board would like to discuss the current $150 (up to 6 hours) plus $250 security deposit and whether it still reflects current costs. Any change would of course be at the board's direction — we'd just like to get your input on where things stand.

Would a short call work to go through these? Happy to work around your schedule.
